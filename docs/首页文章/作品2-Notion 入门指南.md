> 本文档面向 Notion 新手，通过 5 个步骤教会你搭建一个功能完整的任务列表。

## 概述

本指南将带你完成以下操作：

1. 创建 Notion 页面
2. 选择数据库模板
3. 定义任务属性

# Get started

## Creating Your First Task List in Notion



##### Intro: 

This guide walks you through the steps to set up a functional task list to manage your daily projects efficiently.



<font size=4>**Step 1: Create a new page.** Click the `+ Add a page` button in the left sidebar to start a blank canvas, then click `Database` at the bottom. </font>

---

<img src="../images/notion/step1.jpg" alt="step1" style="zoom:60%; align:left; margin-bottom:70px;" />
<br>
<br>


<font size=4>**Step 2: Select a template.** Choose `Projects` from the database options to structure your tasks.</font>

---

<img src="../images/notion/step2.png" alt="step2" style="zoom:60%; align:left; margin-bottom:70px;"/>
<br>
<br>

 <font size=4>**Step 3: Define properties.** Add columns for `Status` (Select), `Assignee` (Person), and `Due Date` (Date).</font>

---

<img src="../images/notion/step3.png" alt="step3" style="zoom: 67%; align:left; margin-bottom:70px;" />
<br>
<br>

<font size=4>**Step 4:  Add your tasks.** Click the `+ New` button at the bottom of the table to input your first item.</font>

---

<img src="../images/notion/step4.png" alt="step4" style="zoom:67%; align:left; margin-bottom:70px;"/>
<br>
<br>

<font size=4>**Step 5:  Switch views.** Choose a layout, such as `Board`, to visualize your task distribution.</font>

---

<img src="../images/notion/step5.jpg" alt="step5" style="zoom:67%; align:left; margin-bottom:70px;"/>
<br>


<img src="../images/notion/step5-2.png" alt="step5-2" style="zoom:50%; align:left; margin-bottom:70px;"/>
<br>
<font size=4>Your task list is now established ! You can now begin adding details to your project tasks.</font>

\> **Tip:** You can drag and drop cards between columns to update their status automatically.
